# LAB 3 Reflection

## Part 1: Synthesis — Building Sound from Math
### 1A
[OBSERVE] Each frequency is double the previous. What happens to the pitch? How would you describe the relationship between the number (Hz) and what you hear?
- The pitch goes higher every time the frequency doubles. So when the number (Hz) increases, the sound becomes higher pitch.
### 1B 
[OBSERVE] All four waves have the same frequency (440 Hz). Do they sound the same pitch? Do they sound like the same instrument? Describe the texture of each.
- They all have the same pitch because they are 440 Hz. But they do not sound the same. The sine wave sounds smooth, the square wave sounds more sharp, the sawtooth sounds very harsh, and the triangle is softer than square. So the texture is different.
### 1C
[OBSERVE] How does the sound change as you add more harmonics? Does the pitch change? What does change?

-  When I add more harmonics, the sound becomes more rich and full. The pitch does not change, but the sound feels thicker and warmer.
## Part 2: Composition — Sequences, Chords, and Beats
### 2B
[OBSERVE] The Am chord uses intervals of 3 and 7 semitones (minor). The C chord uses 4 and 7 (major). Listen carefully — how would you describe the difference between a minor chord and a major chord?

- The minor chord sounds more sad or emotional. The major chord sounds more happy and bright. That is the main difference I hear.
### 2C
[OBSERVE] Listen to the beat. Try turning off the hihat by zeroing its pattern:
hihat_pat = [0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0]

- When I remove the hihat, the beat feels more empty and less active. The hihat makes the beat feel more full and keeps the rhythm.

[OBSERVE] Try moving the snare to a different slot, e.g. snare_pat = [0,0,0,1,0,0,0,1]. What changes?
- When I move the snare, the rhythm changes. It feels different and sometimes off. The position of the snare changes the whole beat feeling.

## Part 3: Effects — Shaping Sound in Post
### 3A
[OBSERVE] How does the echo change the perceived space of the sound? Does it feel like a small room or a large one?

- The echo makes the sound feel like it is in a bigger space. A short delay feels like a small room, and a long delay feels like a large space.
